About Andrée

01 / 06

ANDRÉE builds rooms out of low frequencies — sets composed for dark spaces, close speakers and people who came to disappear for a while.

Founder of the DRKSD collective, based between Prague's basements and warehouse floors, ANDRÉE treats every performance as a single unrepeatable cut — no two sets share a tracklist.

The 20GEMS series documents this philosophy: one set, one location, one live recording — a rough stone taken in, and a finished gem released.

2019

First cut

2021

DRKSD founded
